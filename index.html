🛡️ Prevention Tips & Over-the-Counter (OTC) Relief

  • Peak Hours: Keep windows closed between 5:00 AM and 10:00 AM when pollen and London Plane trichomes peak.
  • Protective Gear: Wear wraparound sunglasses and a mask when walking under plane trees in September–October.
  • Hygiene: Shower and change clothes after spending time outdoors to remove trapped pollen.
  • Common OTC Options: Second-generation oral antihistamines (Loratadine, Cetirizine, Fexofenadine), saline nasal sprays, and lubricating eye drops.

*Disclaimer: Informational guide only. Consult a pharmacist or healthcare professional before taking any medication.

Climate & Weather Impact on Marcos Paz Trees

How heavy rainfall, heatwaves, and Pampean storms affect urban forest health and stability.

☀️ Extreme Heatwaves

Thermal Stress & Drought
Summer Risk

Impact: Prolonged temperatures above 35°C cause leaf scorching, premature defoliation, and soil dehydration—especially in young saplings and shallow-rooted species like Linden (Tilo).

Urban Mitigation: Mature canopies like Tipa and London Plane reduce local street temperatures by up to 4°C through shade and evapotranspiration.

🌧️ Heavy Rainfall & Flooding

Soil Saturation & Root Rot
Soil Stress

Impact: Intense Pampean downpours saturate clay soils in Marcos Paz, reducing root oxygen and softening ground anchorage for tall trees.

Resilient Natives: Riparian species like Ceibo thrive in waterlogged soils, whereas exotic species suffer higher fungal root decay.

💨 Windstorms & Sudden Gusts

Branch Breakage & Uprooting
Structural Hazard

Impact: Strong convective storms and Pampero gusts frequently snap brittle branches in overgrown Eucalyptus and Chinaberry (Paraíso) trees.

Prevention: Scheduled winter crown thinning and replacing high-risk specimens prevent power line damage and street blockages.
