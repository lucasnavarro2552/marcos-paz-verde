🛡️ Prevention Tips & Over-the-Counter (OTC) Relief

  • Peak Hours: Keep windows closed between 5:00 AM and 10:00 AM when pollen and London Plane trichomes peak.
  • Protective Gear: Wear wraparound sunglasses and a mask when walking under plane trees in September–October.
  • Hygiene: Shower and change clothes after spending time outdoors to remove trapped pollen.
  • Common OTC Options: Second-generation oral antihistamines (Loratadine, Cetirizine, Fexofenadine), saline nasal sprays, and lubricating eye drops.

*Disclaimer: Informational guide only. Consult a pharmacist or healthcare professional before taking any medication.
